Privacy Policy for E6B Pro

Effective Date: November 18, 2026
Application Name: E6B Pro
Package ID: com.aistudio.e6bflightcomputer.vxnqpl

1. Overview

E6B Pro ("the App") is an offline-first aviation flight computer and cockpit calculation utility designed for pilots, flight instructors, flight students, and aviation enthusiasts. Because the App operates offline in the cockpit without requiring an internet connection, all flight calculations, aircraft weight-and-balance profiles, navigation logs (NavLogs), and saved logbook entries are stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect and Store

A. Locally Stored User Data (On-Device Only):

  • Aircraft Profiles: Custom aircraft names, tail numbers, weight-and-balance station arms/weights, CG envelope coordinates, fuel burn rates, and cruise airspeeds.
  • Navigation Logs (NavLogs): Waypoint identifiers, altitudes, courses, distances, wind vectors, and fuel calculations.
  • Saved Cockpit Calculations: Snapshots of wind triangle, atmosphere/density altitude, holding pattern, VNAV, or weight-and-balance calculations saved to the in-app logbook.
  • App Preferences: Selected measurement units (Imperial/Metric) and display theme settings stored in local SharedPreferences.

We do not transmit, upload, sync, or back up this data to any external server or cloud database.

B. User-Initiated File Import & Export:

When you use the Import / Export Phone E6B feature to load or save .json or .csv files, the App uses the zero-permission Android System File Picker (Storage Access Framework). The App only accesses the specific file you explicitly choose to open or save.

C. No Personal Identifiers, Location, or Telemetry:

  • No Account Required: E6B Pro does not require user registration, login credentials, names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Location Tracking: The App does not access or track your device's GPS or background/foreground location.
  • No Analytics or Advertising SDKs: The App contains no third-party advertising networks, behavioral trackers, or analytics SDKs.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because E6B Pro does not collect or transmit any user data off your device, we never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or flight data with third parties. Your data leaves your device only if you explicitly export a .json backup file or copy an export payload to your clipboard and share it yourself.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All data created within E6B Pro remains on your device under your sole control. You can delete individual NavLog legs, custom aircraft profiles, or saved logbook calculations directly within the App at any time. Uninstalling E6B Pro or clearing the App's storage in Android system settings permanently deletes all locally stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

E6B Pro does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Operational Aviation Disclaimer

E6B Pro is designed as a supplemental situational-awareness, flight-training, and planning aid. Pilots in Command (PIC) must always verify all weight-and-balance, performance, fuel, and navigation calculations against official FAA/EASA-approved Pilot's Operating Handbooks (POH), Aircraft Flight Manuals (AFM), and current aeronautical charts prior to flight.
